Frequently asked questions

How do I open the drawing panel?

Click the Sketch Launch menu bar icon, or use the global keyboard shortcut. The default shortcut is ⌥⌘G, and you can change it in Settings.

How do I draw a gesture?

With a trackpad, click the drawing area and draw with one finger. With a mouse, hold the left button and draw in the box. You can lift and draw additional strokes before the recognition pause.

What can a gesture do?

Assign a gesture to launch an app, open a URL, run a saved script, or run an Apple Shortcut. You can test the action from the gesture editor.

How many gestures can I create?

The free version supports up to five gestures. A one-time in-app purchase unlocks unlimited gestures. Existing gestures remain available if you reach the free limit.

How do I restore my purchase?

Open Settings in Sketch Launch and choose Restore Purchases. Make sure you are signed in with the Apple Account used for the purchase.
